PM-WANI Wi-Fi Network & PDO Hub

Prime Minister Wi-Fi Access Network Interface — Elevating Wireless Internet Connectivity Across India

Govt. of India Initiative

Turn Your Internet Connection into Earnings

The Prime Minister Wi-Fi Access Network Interface (PM-WANI) is a transformative national initiative aimed at expanding high-speed wireless internet access through Public Wi-Fi Hotspots.

Through Thagadur WiFi PM-WANI, small shopkeepers, tea stalls, local businesses, and individuals can register as **Public Data Offices (PDO)**. By installing a PDO Wi-Fi device, you can provide Wi-Fi access to visitors and earn steady income effortlessly.

Become a PDO Partner

Why Join Thagadur PM-WANI?

  • Seamless Setup & Provisioning of PDO Devices
  • Boost income for small shopkeepers & local businesses
  • Affordable, high-speed data packages for youth & community
  • Secure HTTPS integration with PDO Aggregators (PDOA)
  • Full tech support & troubleshooting from Thagadur Team
