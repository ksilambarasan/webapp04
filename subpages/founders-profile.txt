Meet Our Director
Kalpana Kandhasamy
Founder and Director of Thagadur Career Services
Email :
[email protected]
Empowering Careers, Inspiring Change
With over 10 years of experience in the training and placement sector, Kalpana Kandhasamy is a passionate advocate for talent development and career advancement. As the founder and director of Thagadur Career Services, she is committed to fostering an inclusive environment where individuals can thrive.
Education and Expertise
Kalpana Kandhasamy holds a Master of Engineering in VLSI Design. Her educational background, combined with extensive hands-on experience, equips her with a unique understanding of the skills and qualities that employers seek.
Professional Journey
Starting her career as Director, She quickly recognized the need for effective training and support in the job market. She has since held various leadership positions in renowned organizations, driving initiatives that empower candidates and enhance their employability.
Vision and Impact
Under Kalpana Kandhasamy ‘s leadership. She believes in the potential of every individual and works tirelessly to ensure that everyone has access to the resources and guidance they need to succeed.
Commitment to Empowerment
Kalpana Kandhasamy  is particularly passionate about supporting women and marginalized groups, advocating for equal opportunities in the workforce. Her initiatives have helped numerous candidates build confidence and acquire the skills necessary for thriving careers.
Connect with Kalpana Kandhasamy
If you’re ready to take the next step in your career journey or seek guidance, Kalpana is here to help. Reach out today to explore how Thagadur Career Services can support your aspirations.
