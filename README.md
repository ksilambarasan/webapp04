# Thagadur Career Services Website

Thagadur Career Services is a modern, dark-glassmorphic Single Page Application (SPA) built for **Thagadur Career Services** (Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu). Based on authentic company data, the platform combines placement consultancy, staffing solutions, Hire-Train-Deploy services, Job-Oriented Training, and Central Govt **PM-WANI Wi-Fi Public Data Office (PDO)** network management.

## 🚀 Key Offerings & Features

- **PM-WANI Wi-Fi Public Data Office (PDO) Hub**: Empowers small businesses and shopkeepers to host Wi-Fi hotspots and earn passive income under the PM-WANI scheme.
- **Hire, Train, Deploy**: A 3-step talent management service sourcing top candidates, delivering targeted job-ready skill training, and deploying professionals.
- **Staffing Services**: Tailored candidate matching, resume optimization, interview coaching, and permanent/contract staffing solutions for employers.
- **Job-Oriented Training**: Skill coaching for graduates and professionals preparing for competitive technical and corporate job markets.
- **Founder & Director Profile**: Highlighting Founder **Kalpana Kandhasamy** (M.E. in VLSI Design, 10+ years in training & placement).
- **Official Head Office**: Located on Pennagram Road, opposite DDCC Bank, Dharmapuri - 636701, Tamil Nadu. (Phone: `+91-7899752753`, Email: `info@thagadur.com`).

```bash

```
Then navigate to `http://localhost:8000` in your web browser.
